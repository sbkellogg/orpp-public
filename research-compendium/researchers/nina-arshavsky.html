Research report2020 · NC-009

What Happens When You Combine High School and College? The Impact of Early Colleges on Postsecondary Performance and Completion

Julie A. Edmunds; Fatih Unlu; Jane Furey; Elizabeth Glennie; Nina Arshavsky

Lottery-offered students more often earned a postsecondary credential (44.3% versus 33.0%), particularly an associate degree. The overall bachelor’s completion difference at six years was not statistically significant.

Working paper2026 · NC-039

The Role of Course Delivery Modality in Dual Enrollment

Julie A. Edmunds; Brian Phillips; Christine Mulhern; Nina Arshavsky; Bryan C. Hutchins

All three delivery groups had favorable postsecondary enrollment and credential estimates versus nonparticipants. Course pass rates differed; online CTE had the lowest pass rates.

Research brief2023 · NC-041

Why Do Students Participate (or Not) in Dual Enrollment?

Bryan C. Hutchins; Nina Arshavsky; Julie Edmunds; Laura Rosof

Participants valued savings, challenge, and career exploration. Awareness, advising, remaining costs, transportation, scheduling, and course availability could limit participation.
