Journal article2020 · NC-038

Social Returns to Private Choice? Effects of Charter Schools on Behavioral Outcomes, Arrests, and Civic Participation

Andrew McEachin; Douglas Lee Lauen; Sarah Crittenden Fuller; Rachel M. Perera

Students entering charter schools from traditional public schools had favorable absence, suspension, conviction, and voting estimates. Results were less consistent for students already in the charter sector.
