What we know—and what remains open

Priority 1EmergingRA-004

Eligibility alone does not ensure access.

Advanced learning access

CCP research adds delivery and participation evidence: advising, remaining costs, schedules, and transportation shape opportunities. These qualitative findings complement, rather than establish, effects of changing access rules.

Priority 1EmergingRA-005

Pathway benefits vary by model and outcome.

Postsecondary pathways

Early-college lottery evidence supports credential attainment. CCP analyses add favorable education and recorded-earnings associations, with selection and employment-data limits. They do not establish that one pathway or delivery format is best.

Priority 2EmergingRA-009

Working conditions connect to retention and teachers’ plans.

Working conditions

Earlier NC research links workplace conditions to observed retention. The 2026 TWC report adds descriptive evidence about intended career plans; rural interviews provide context. These different measures should be interpreted separately.

Staffing measures need to reflect team responsibilities.

Qualifications and staffing

Teacher-assistant and ATR studies address different staffing arrangements. The ATR class-size report proposes team ratios that include release time, but its small one-year analysis cannot establish class-size effects.

Priority 2EmergingRA-011

Professional learning needs support for classroom application.

Professional learning and leadership

ATR and coaching sources examine different forms of support. New LETRS observations describe instructional strengths and remaining problems after training, without estimating training effects. ALTER still needs pilot validation; CARES findings remain preliminary.

Priority 3EmergingRA-014

Program results depend on the version and outcome evaluated.

Effectiveness

The collection now includes early Read to Achieve and Opportunity Scholarship evaluations, early-childhood funding research, and ATR and Restart studies. Their populations, policy periods, and designs differ; agency summaries are not independent impact replications.

Priority 3EmergingRA-015

Implementation studies identify barriers that outcome averages can miss.

Implementation

LETRS observations, CCP interviews, and preliminary coaching reports describe how support and access work in practice. Such evidence helps define improvement questions but does not establish that a proposed change causes better outcomes. ATR annual case studies add district-specific evidence about roles and coaching time.

Research report2026 · NC-033

The Selection and Evaluation of Advanced Teachers: Summative Report

Sarah Bausell; James Birkett; Duncan Culbreth; Brayan Diaz; Shaun Kellogg; Emily Thrasher; Robin Anderson; Lam Pham; Tamara Young; Kari Colley; Sarah Dyson; Caitlin Kearney; Liv Rau; Matthew G. Springer; David Stuit

Districts used varied, multistage selection processes; evaluation practices were less developed and often missed teacher-leadership responsibilities. No single selection measure consistently predicted both student achievement and supported-teacher retention. The study developed the Adult Leadership Teacher Evaluation Rubric (ALTER).
