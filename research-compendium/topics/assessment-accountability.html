Priority 2EmergingRA-009

Working conditions connect to retention and teachers’ plans.

Working conditions

Earlier NC research links workplace conditions to observed retention. The 2026 TWC report adds descriptive evidence about intended career plans; rural interviews provide context. These different measures should be interpreted separately.

School climate

Working-conditions research, the 2026 TWC report, and a community-school pilot provide complementary perspectives. Staff perceptions and intentions should be distinguished from measured workforce and student outcomes.

2026 North Carolina Teacher Working Conditions Survey: Preliminary Statewide Findings and Implications for School, District, and State Leaders
