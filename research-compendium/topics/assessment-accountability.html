What we know—and what remains open

Priority 1EmergingRA-006

School-sector results differ across outcomes and entry decisions.

School-sector pathways

Charter research adds behavioral and civic outcomes for historical ninth-grade entrants. The early Opportunity Scholarship study reports positive estimates with strong volunteer-sample and test-comparability limits. Neither establishes current sector-wide superiority.

Priority 2EmergingRA-009

Working conditions connect to retention and teachers’ plans.

Working conditions

Earlier NC research links workplace conditions to observed retention. The 2026 TWC report adds descriptive evidence about intended career plans; rural interviews provide context. These different measures should be interpreted separately.

Priority 3EmergingRA-014

Program results depend on the version and outcome evaluated.

Effectiveness

The collection now includes early Read to Achieve and Opportunity Scholarship evaluations, early-childhood funding research, and ATR and Restart studies. Their populations, policy periods, and designs differ; agency summaries are not independent impact replications.

Priority 4EmergingRA-044

Survey findings and local accounts offer different views of climate.

School climate

Working-conditions research, the 2026 TWC report, and a community-school pilot provide complementary perspectives. Staff perceptions and intentions should be distinguished from measured workforce and student outcomes.

Priority 5EmergingRA-048

Comparable measurement is central to credible comparisons.

Assessment selection and use

The Opportunity Scholarship assessment review is complemented by a historical achievement study whose estimates depend partly on the test family used by participating schools. That study does not identify one universally best assessment.

Priority 5EmergingRA-052

Useful reporting requires clear comparisons and calculation rules.

Accountability reporting

The assessment review addresses cross-sector comparisons. The 2026 TWC report provides a workforce-reporting example, documenting response denominators and aggregation. These sources address distinct reporting needs.

Priority 6EmergingRA-056

Access policies can shape who enters an opportunity.

Student access and opportunity

Mathematics, school-choice, and CCP work address different entry processes. New CCP interviews describe practical access barriers; the historical voucher analysis has a selective sample and does not establish outcomes under current eligibility rules.

Agency report2026 · NC-032

2026 North Carolina Teacher Working Conditions Survey: Preliminary Statewide Findings and Implications for School, District, and State Leaders

Shaun Kellogg

Teachers reported strong school commitment, with 84.3% planning to stay at their school. Time, student conduct, and professional support were weaker areas; ratings differed by intended career plans.

Journal article2020 · NC-036

An Analysis of the Effects of North Carolina’s Opportunity Scholarship Program on Student Achievement

Anna J. Egalite; D. T. Stallings; Stephen R. Porter

New scholarship recipients had positive mathematics and language estimates, but no detectable reading effect. Positive estimates were concentrated in private schools using the same test family.
