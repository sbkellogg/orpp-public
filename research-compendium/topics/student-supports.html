What we know—and what remains open

Priority 3EmergingRA-015

Implementation studies identify barriers that outcome averages can miss.

Implementation

LETRS observations, CCP interviews, and preliminary coaching reports describe how support and access work in practice. Such evidence helps define improvement questions but does not establish that a proposed change causes better outcomes. ATR annual case studies add district-specific evidence about roles and coaching time.

Journal article2025 · NC-002

In School, Engaged, on Track? The Effect of the Pandemic on Student Attendance, Course Grades, and Grade Retention in North Carolina

Sarah Crittenden Fuller; Tom Swiderski; Camille Mikkelsen; Kevin C. Bastian

Attendance, course grades, and grade progression worsened during the pandemic. Disruptions were uneven, with larger difficulties for some middle-grade and historically marginalized student groups.

Journal article2024 · NC-005

School-Based Healthcare and Absenteeism: Evidence from Telemedicine

Sarah Komisarow; Steven W. Hemelt

Access to school telemedicine was associated with a 2.5 percentage-point reduction in chronic absence and about 0.8 fewer days absent; effects were concentrated among male students.

Ending Early Grade Suspensions
