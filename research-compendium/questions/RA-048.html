Priority 5EmergingRA-048 · Supporting Question

Assessment selection and use

What are the relative strengths, limitations, feasibility, and instructional implications of different assessment and screening approaches?

Draft evidence synthesis

Comparable measurement is central to credible comparisons.

The Opportunity Scholarship assessment review is complemented by a historical achievement study whose estimates depend partly on the test family used by participating schools. That study does not identify one universally best assessment.

Based on NC-020, NC-036.

Considerations for practice

Specify the interpretation and check assessment alignment.

What we still need to learn

How can representative comparisons use feasible, comparable measures?

Recommendations for North Carolina’s Opportunity Scholarship Program, Pursuant to S.L. 2024-57

Matthew Pepper; Anna Egalite; Matthew G. Springer

The report identifies tradeoffs among comparability, testing burden, and autonomy. Scores from different tests should not be treated as directly comparable across sectors.

Journal article2020 · NC-036

An Analysis of the Effects of North Carolina’s Opportunity Scholarship Program on Student Achievement

Anna J. Egalite; D. T. Stallings; Stephen R. Porter

New scholarship recipients had positive mathematics and language estimates, but no detectable reading effect. Positive estimates were concentrated in private schools using the same test family.
