Implementation

How do implementation, dosage, fidelity, staffing, leadership, educator workload, and local context influence effectiveness?

Draft evidence synthesis

Implementation evidence helps explain what to examine next.

Preliminary LETRS and tutoring records identify delivery and support issues. They should guide questions for local implementation, while stronger outcome evaluation continues.

Based on NC-021, NC-022.

Considerations for practice

Track time, staffing, participation, and instructional use.

What we still need to learn

Link implementation measures to credible outcome estimates.
