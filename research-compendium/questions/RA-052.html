Priority 5EmergingRA-052 · Supporting Question

Accountability reporting

How should assessment, accountability, and school climate or workforce indicators, including Teacher Working Conditions (TWC) results, be reported so meaningful differences, trends, and school profiles remain visible and useful?

Draft evidence synthesis

Useful reporting requires clear comparisons and calculation rules.

The assessment review addresses cross-sector comparisons. The 2026 TWC report provides a workforce-reporting example, documenting response denominators and aggregation. These sources address distinct reporting needs.

Based on NC-020, NC-032.

Considerations for practice

Make calculation rules, missing responses, and subgroup coverage visible alongside results.

What we still need to learn

Evaluate which reporting approaches support valid local decisions.
