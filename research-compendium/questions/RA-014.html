Priority 3EmergingRA-014 · Supporting Question

Effectiveness

What effects are associated with major state, local, and federally funded (e.g., Title I-IX) initiatives, policies, programs, and activities, including their effects on educator working conditions, retention, and student outcomes?

Draft evidence synthesis

Effects vary across programs and outcomes.

The initial collection includes evaluations of ATR and Restart. These are separate models with different outcome patterns, not a head-to-head comparison of investment choices.

Based on NC-016, NC-018.

Considerations for practice

Specify the outcome and local conditions before applying findings.

What we still need to learn

Add comparable cost and implementation evidence.

2026 Advanced Teaching Roles Evaluation Report

Journal article2025 · NC-018

Beyond Prescriptive Reforms: An Examination of North Carolina’s Flexible School Restart Program

Lam D. Pham; Gage F. Matthews; Timothy A. Drake

Restart showed positive mathematics effects, but no detected English language arts or non-test outcome effects. Lower teacher and principal turnover accounted for part of the mathematics estimate.
