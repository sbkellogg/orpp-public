Supply and stability

Where are vacancies, attrition, mobility, and staffing shortages concentrated across educators, school leaders, and specialized instructional support personnel, and which working conditions best predict subsequent workforce instability across educator experience levels and school contexts?

Draft evidence synthesis

Statewide indicators and rural accounts answer different workforce questions.

The agency summary provides recent attrition measures; rural interviews add local perspectives on why retention can be difficult. Together they identify issues for follow-up rather than proving a retention strategy works.

Based on NC-017, NC-023.

Considerations for practice

Pair local staffing data with structured staff conversations.

What we still need to learn

Identify persistent shortages and evaluate responses.
