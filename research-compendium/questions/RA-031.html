Priority 3EmergingRA-031 · Area of Interest

School health and behavioral health initiatives

Examine how school health and behavioral health initiatives affect access to services, student well-being, attendance, and learning, including differences across schools and student groups.

Draft evidence synthesis

Healthcare access may be one route to stronger attendance.

A rural telemedicine study offers evidence about school-based healthcare access. It does not establish the effectiveness of all behavioral-health initiatives.

Based on NC-005.

Considerations for practice

Assess local service barriers and partnerships.

What we still need to learn

Examine costs and effects across additional NC settings.

Journal article2024 · NC-005

School-Based Healthcare and Absenteeism: Evidence from Telemedicine

Sarah Komisarow; Steven W. Hemelt

Access to school telemedicine was associated with a 2.5 percentage-point reduction in chronic absence and about 0.8 fewer days absent; effects were concentrated among male students.
