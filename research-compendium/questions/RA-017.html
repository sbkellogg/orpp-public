Priority 3EmergingRA-017 · Supporting Question

Cost and return on investment

What are the costs associated with different approaches relative to their outcomes, and when do benefits justify costs?

Draft evidence synthesis

Effectiveness and affordability need to be examined together.

Teacher-assistant research offers historical staffing evidence. The tutoring project is investigating delivery models, but a complete cost comparison was not reviewed here.

Based on NC-022, NC-029.

Considerations for practice

Request current cost and staffing assumptions before scaling.

What we still need to learn

Comparable contemporary cost-per-outcome estimates remain a search priority.
