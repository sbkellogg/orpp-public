Priority 6EmergingRA-055 · Supporting Question

Resource allocation and school finance

How do alternative funding formulas, allotment structures, and resource allocation policies affect the distribution, adequacy, flexibility, and use of educational resources across schools and districts?

Draft evidence synthesis

Allocation evidence should connect resources, needs, and outcomes.

The initial sources cover historical finance analysis, teacher-assistant staffing, and planned ESSER priorities. These measures do not provide a current causal comparison of funding formulas.

Based on NC-019, NC-027, NC-029.

Considerations for practice

Distinguish planned spending, actual staffing, and outcomes.

What we still need to learn

Add recent distribution analyses and evaluated policy changes.
