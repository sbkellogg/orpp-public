Priority 6EmergingRA-066 · Area of Interest

Innovative school models and operational flexibility

Examine how innovative school models use operational flexibility and how those choices relate to educational opportunities, organizational capacity, and student outcomes.

Draft evidence synthesis

Restart provides one NC example of operational flexibility.

Its outcome pattern varies across subjects and measures. The model should be studied as implemented, rather than equated with every innovative-school approach.

Based on NC-018.

Considerations for practice

Document how flexibility changes instruction and staffing.

What we still need to learn

Which implementation choices explain differences across schools?
