Priority 2EmergingRA-010 · Supporting Question

Qualifications and staffing

How are licensure, certification, staffing ratios, caseloads, and other workforce characteristics associated with student and organizational outcomes?

Draft evidence synthesis

Support roles can contribute to student outcomes.

The teacher-assistant analysis provides NC evidence on one staffing category. It does not establish the effects of every ratio, caseload, credential, or staffing arrangement.

Based on NC-029.

Considerations for practice

Examine responsibilities as well as headcounts.

What we still need to learn

Compare modern staffing models and implementation.
