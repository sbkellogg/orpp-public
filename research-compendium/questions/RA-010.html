Priority 2EmergingRA-010 · Supporting Question

Qualifications and staffing

How are licensure, certification, staffing ratios, caseloads, and other workforce characteristics associated with student and organizational outcomes?

Draft evidence synthesis

Staffing measures need to reflect team responsibilities.

Teacher-assistant and ATR studies address different staffing arrangements. The ATR class-size report proposes team ratios that include release time, but its small one-year analysis cannot establish class-size effects.

Based on NC-029, NC-033, NC-043.

Considerations for practice

Record team roles, caseloads, and release time alongside headcounts.

What we still need to learn

Validate staffing measures and examine outcomes across years.

Journal article2021 · NC-029

Do Teacher Assistants Improve Student Outcomes? Evidence From School Funding Cutbacks in North Carolina

Steven W. Hemelt; Helen F. Ladd; Calen R. Clifton

The analysis finds positive teacher-assistant effects on reading and mathematics, with larger benefits for students of color and students in high-poverty schools.

Research report2026 · NC-033

The Selection and Evaluation of Advanced Teachers: Summative Report

Sarah Bausell; James Birkett; Duncan Culbreth; Brayan Diaz; Shaun Kellogg; Emily Thrasher; Robin Anderson; Lam Pham; Tamara Young; Kari Colley; Sarah Dyson; Caitlin Kearney; Liv Rau; Matthew G. Springer; David Stuit

Districts used varied, multistage selection processes; evaluation practices were less developed and often missed teacher-leadership responsibilities. No single selection measure consistently predicted both student achievement and supported-teacher retention. The study developed the Adult Leadership Teacher Evaluation Rubric (ALTER).

Agency report2025 · NC-043

Advanced Teaching Roles Class Size Report

North Carolina Department of Public Instruction

The report proposes team-level student-to-teacher ratios that account for leadership release time. A small, one-year Classroom Excellence analysis found no clear class-size relationship with teacher growth scores.
