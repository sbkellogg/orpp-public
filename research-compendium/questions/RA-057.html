Priority 6EmergingRA-057 · Supporting Question

Workforce and organizational flexibility

How do policies governing educator qualifications, staffing, planning time, leadership roles, educator voice, and operational authority influence working conditions, recruitment, retention, instructional capacity, and school effectiveness?

Draft evidence synthesis

Operational flexibility requires clear roles and measurement.

ATR and Restart studies address specific models. The separate ATR staffing report explains team ratios and data needs; it does not establish that larger classes are harmless or that a ratio rule improves outcomes.

Based on NC-016, NC-018, NC-033, NC-043, NC-044.

Considerations for practice

Document staffing decisions, release time, and actual implementation.

What we still need to learn

Test current role and staffing approaches with stronger longitudinal evidence.

Research report2026 · NC-033

The Selection and Evaluation of Advanced Teachers: Summative Report

Sarah Bausell; James Birkett; Duncan Culbreth; Brayan Diaz; Shaun Kellogg; Emily Thrasher; Robin Anderson; Lam Pham; Tamara Young; Kari Colley; Sarah Dyson; Caitlin Kearney; Liv Rau; Matthew G. Springer; David Stuit

Districts used varied, multistage selection processes; evaluation practices were less developed and often missed teacher-leadership responsibilities. No single selection measure consistently predicted both student achievement and supported-teacher retention. The study developed the Adult Leadership Teacher Evaluation Rubric (ALTER).
