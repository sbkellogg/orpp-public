Scheduling and delivery models

Explore how school schedules and instructional delivery models relate to learning opportunities, student outcomes, and staffing needs, and which approaches work best in different settings.

Draft evidence synthesis

Delivery formats offer opportunities with different support needs.

The NC dual-enrollment modality abstract reports favorable postsecondary outcomes across formats versus nonparticipation, while pass rates differ. It is not a randomized comparison of online and in-person provision.

Based on NC-039.

Considerations for practice

Pair delivery choices with course-success supports.

Working paper2026 · NC-039

The Role of Course Delivery Modality in Dual Enrollment

Julie A. Edmunds; Brian Phillips; Christine Mulhern; Nina Arshavsky; Bryan C. Hutchins

All three delivery groups had favorable postsecondary enrollment and credential estimates versus nonparticipants. Course pass rates differed; online CTE had the lowest pass rates.
