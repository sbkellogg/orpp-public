Priority 2EmergingRA-011 · Supporting Question

Professional learning and leadership

Which professional learning, coaching, leadership, and role-design approaches improve educator practice and support, and how do leadership, time, safety, and professional learning interact to influence workforce outcomes?

Draft evidence synthesis

Professional support shows promise, with uneven outcome evidence.

The indexed ATR evaluation and coaching summary address different forms of support. Achievement, retention, and implementation findings vary; a project description without results is also indexed separately.

Based on NC-016, NC-021, NC-024.

Considerations for practice

Document how support is delivered and which outcomes it targets.

What we still need to learn

Which coaching and role features explain sustained benefits?
