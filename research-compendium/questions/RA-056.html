Priority 6EmergingRA-056 · Supporting Question

Student access and opportunity

How do enrollment, assignment, eligibility, and participation policies influence access to educational opportunities, and what are their consequences for student pathways and outcomes?

Draft evidence synthesis

Access policies can shape who enters an opportunity.

Indexed mathematics and charter-sector work highlights different entry and participation processes. Findings are specific to the policy and period studied.

Based on NC-014, NC-028.

Considerations for practice

Review eligibility rules alongside enrollment composition.

What we still need to learn

Update policy effects using recent cohorts.
