Priority 6EmergingRA-059 · Supporting Question

Distributional effects and policy tradeoffs

How would proposed policy changes affect different students, schools, districts, and communities, and what tradeoffs arise among competing policy objectives?

Draft evidence synthesis

Policy tradeoffs include access, resources, and community effects.

The indexed finance review and immigration-enforcement study examine distinct distributional issues. They inform selected dimensions rather than a comprehensive ranking of policies.

Based on NC-008, NC-027.

Considerations for practice

Specify which students and communities a decision may affect.

What we still need to learn

Add current NC evidence about alternative policies.
