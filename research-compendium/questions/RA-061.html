Weighted-student funding and alternative state school finance models

Compare how weighted-student funding and alternative school finance models distribute resources across districts and student needs, including implications for equity, adequacy, and local planning.

Draft evidence synthesis

Funding design needs a current distributional analysis.

The indexed 2019 finance study offers a framework for considering adequacy and alignment. It does not simulate a current weighted-student formula.
