Priority 6EmergingRA-060 · Supporting Question

Policy monitoring and evaluation

How can longitudinal data, comparative analyses, and appropriate evaluation designs inform decisions about maintaining, modifying, expanding, or discontinuing policies?

Draft evidence synthesis

Monitoring requires consistent definitions and follow-through.

Agency workforce reporting and ESSER-plan analysis illustrate useful descriptive monitoring. Descriptive trends and plans alone cannot determine whether a policy caused improvement.

Based on NC-017, NC-019.

Considerations for practice

Link plans, implementation, and subsequent outcomes.

What we still need to learn

Which monitoring systems support credible continuation decisions?
