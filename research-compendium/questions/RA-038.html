Studies and projects connected to this question

Research report2020 · NC-009

What Happens When You Combine High School and College? The Impact of Early Colleges on Postsecondary Performance and Completion

SERVE Center research team

Lottery-offered students more often earned a postsecondary credential (44.3% versus 33.0%), particularly an associate degree. The overall bachelor’s completion difference at six years was not statistically significant.

Working paper2022 · NC-010

CTE-Focused Dual Enrollment: Participation and Outcomes

Julie A. Edmunds; Fatih Unlu; Brian Phillips; Bryan C. Hutchins; Christine Mulhern

About 9% participated. Participation was positively associated with college credits, high-school graduation, and college enrollment; participation disparities were smaller than in college-transfer dual enrollment.
