Career and College Promise College Transfer Pathway: Postsecondary Attainment and Employment Outcomes

CCP Evaluation Partnership (UNC Greensboro, RAND, and NC agency partners)

Postsecondary & CTE

What the study found

The briefs report favorable college-enrollment and credential outcomes. A later employment brief reports higher recorded annual and cumulative wages for College Transfer participants.

Considerations for practice

Follow both education and employment outcomes when examining pathway benefits.

Limitations and interpretation

Briefs give limited technical detail. Nonrandom participation remains a concern. NC unemployment-insurance records omit out-of-state, federal, and self-employment; missing wage records are coded zero.

Questions that remain

How sensitive are results to unobserved selection and missing employment sectors?

Study at a glance

Design
Statistically adjusted participant–nonparticipant comparisons
NC setting & population
Postsecondary brief: NC high-school cohorts from 2012–19. Earnings brief: about 232,000 students entering grade 11 in 2012–14.

Pathway benefits vary by model and outcome.

Postsecondary pathways

Early-college lottery evidence supports credential attainment. CCP analyses add favorable education and recorded-earnings associations, with selection and employment-data limits. They do not establish that one pathway or delivery format is best.
