2024 Advanced Teaching Roles Annual Evaluation Report

Sarah Bausell; Shaun Kellogg; Emily Thrasher; Lam Pham; Tamara Young

Educator workforceWorking ConditionsInstructional improvement

What the study found

School-level estimates were positive and significant in mathematics, while overall ELA, science, and teacher-turnover differences were not significant. Gains were stronger in earlier implementation cohorts. District case studies identified role clarity and release-time challenges.

Considerations for practice

Clarify responsibilities and protect time for coaching while tracking outcomes over several implementation years.

Limitations and interpretation

School comparisons are nonrandomized and average across varied models. Teacher-level results use one year and a small PSU sample and are explicitly non-causal. Voluntary surveys and two district case studies limit generalization.

Questions that remain

Which coaching arrangements explain sustained gains and variation across districts?

Study at a glance

Design
Mixed methods; matched staggered difference-in-differences and district case studies
NC setting & population
NC ATR programs: 2023–24 implementation across 17 PSUs, Wilson and Pitt case studies, and impact analysis through 2022–23.

Priority 3EmergingRA-015

Implementation studies identify barriers that outcome averages can miss.

Implementation

LETRS observations, CCP interviews, and preliminary coaching reports describe how support and access work in practice. Such evidence helps define improvement questions but does not establish that a proposed change causes better outcomes. ATR annual case studies add district-specific evidence about roles and coaching time.

Priority 6EmergingRA-057

Operational flexibility requires clear roles and measurement.

Workforce and organizational flexibility

ATR and Restart studies address specific models. The separate ATR staffing report explains team ratios and data needs; it does not establish that larger classes are harmless or that a ratio rule improves outcomes.
