Long-Term Effects of Early Childhood Programs Through Eighth Grade: Do the Effects Fade Out or Grow?

Yu Bai; Helen F. Ladd; Clara G. Muschkin; Kenneth A. Dodge

Early learningLiteracyMathematics

What the study found

Higher funding exposure was associated with better reading and mathematics outcomes and less special-education placement and grade repetition through middle school. More at Four associations were larger for economically disadvantaged students.

Considerations for practice

Consider sustained outcomes and the surrounding early-childhood system when reviewing pre-K investment.

Limitations and interpretation

Observational funding exposure is not individual enrollment or random assignment. Other county changes remain possible explanations; More at Four operated alongside Smart Start.

Questions that remain

Do benefits persist in recent cohorts and later schooling?

Study at a glance

Design
County- and cohort-fixed-effects funding analysis
