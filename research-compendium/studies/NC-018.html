Beyond Prescriptive Reforms: An Examination of North Carolina’s Flexible School Restart Program

Lam D. Pham; Gage F. Matthews; Timothy A. Drake

Instructional improvementSchool finance

What the study found

Restart showed positive mathematics effects, but no detected English language arts or non-test outcome effects. Lower teacher and principal turnover accounted for part of the mathematics estimate.

Considerations for practice

Examine how schools use flexibility and retain staff, rather than assuming autonomy alone produces improvement.

Limitations and interpretation

The comparison design depends on counterfactual assumptions. Results vary across outcomes and do not isolate every component of Restart.

Questions that remain

Which local uses of flexibility are most productive?
