2024–25 State of the Teaching Profession and School Administrator Report: NCDPI Findings Summary

North Carolina Department of Public Instruction

Educator workforceData systems

What the study found

NCDPI reports teacher attrition of 10.11%, compared with 9.88% the previous year. The statutory vacancy measure includes some positions filled by temporarily licensed teachers or rehired retirees.

Considerations for practice

Separate departures, movement within the system, and unstaffed positions when planning workforce responses.

Limitations and interpretation

This record reviews the agency’s public summary, not the full report. Definitions limit comparisons across years and with other states.

Questions that remain

Where do persistent local shortages and early-career departures concentrate?
