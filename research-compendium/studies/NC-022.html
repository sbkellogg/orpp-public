High-Dosage Tutoring for Learning Recovery in Union County

Douglas Lauen and research team

MathematicsStudent supportsInstructional improvement

What the study found

The public summary reports preliminary proficiency gains of 8–10 percentage points relative to comparison cohorts, with positive participant feedback.

Considerations for practice

Examine scheduling, tutor staffing, participation, and whole-grade versus targeted delivery.

Limitations and interpretation

The project page does not provide a full technical analysis or uncertainty estimates. Preliminary gains should not be read as a confirmed causal effect or proven return on investment.

Questions that remain

Which model is effective and affordable across NC contexts?

Study at a glance

Design
Pilot evaluation; comparison cohorts
NC setting & population
Fourth-grade mathematics tutoring in nine Union County elementary schools; whole-grade and targeted models.
