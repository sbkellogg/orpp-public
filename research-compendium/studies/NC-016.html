2026 Advanced Teaching Roles Evaluation Report

Friday Institute evaluation team; authors and contributors listed in the report

Educator workforceWorking ConditionsInstructional improvement

What the study found

The evaluation reports positive schoolwide achievement estimates, strongest in mathematics, but no significant schoolwide turnover effect. Supported-teacher results and release-time patterns were less consistent.

Considerations for practice

Specify coaching responsibilities and monitor implementation alongside several outcomes.

Limitations and interpretation

School comparisons are nonrandomized. The report explicitly describes its two-year teacher-level analyses as correlational, not causal.

Questions that remain

Which staffing and coaching arrangements explain variation across cohorts?

Study at a glance

Mixed methods; school comparison and teacher associations

NC Advanced Teaching Roles schools; impact analysis through 2024–25 and implementation evidence from 2025–26.
