Examining the Small-Group Reading Instruction of K–5 Teachers Who Completed Science of Reading-Aligned Professional Development

Alicia A. Stewart; Paola Pilonieta; Erin K. Washburn; Brittany S. Hart

LiteracyInstructional improvementEducator workforce

What the study found

Lessons included explicit instruction and modeling, alongside continued practices inconsistent with reading research. Observed differences by grade and reading level were not statistically significant.

Considerations for practice

Pair professional learning with observation, feedback, and support for classroom application.

Limitations and interpretation

Volunteers and limited lesson samples restrict generalization. There was no pretraining comparison, so the study cannot estimate LETRS effects; the observation tool also needs validation.

Questions that remain

Which implementation supports improve instruction and student reading?

Study at a glance

Design
Descriptive classroom-video observation
NC setting & population
47 volunteer teachers from 33 NC districts; 83 K–5 lesson videos after LETRS completion.
