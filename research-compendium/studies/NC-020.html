Recommendations for North Carolina’s Opportunity Scholarship Program, Pursuant to S.L. 2024-57

Matthew Pepper; Anna Egalite; Matthew G. Springer

Assessment & accountabilitySchool choice & charter

What the study found

The report identifies tradeoffs among comparability, testing burden, and autonomy. Scores from different tests should not be treated as directly comparable across sectors.

Considerations for practice

Check test comparability and alignment before making cross-sector performance claims.

Limitations and interpretation

This is an assessment-design study, not an estimate of the scholarship program’s effect on learning. Recommendations are those of the authors.

Questions that remain

What assessment information can be reported validly and feasibly?

Study at a glance
