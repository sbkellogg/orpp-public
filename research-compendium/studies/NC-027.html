A Study of Cost Adequacy, Distribution, and Alignment of Funding for North Carolina’s K–12 Public Education System

Jason Willis; Kelsey Krausen; Alex B. Jacobson; Lori Taylor; Ruthie Caparas; Ryan W. Lewis; Karina Jaquet

School finance

What the study found

The study treats funding adequacy, distribution, and alignment to student needs as connected issues. Authors recommend greater attention to higher-need students, flexibility, and predictable investment.

Considerations for practice

Evaluate allocation designs against student needs and local capacity as well as total dollars.

Limitations and interpretation

The reviewed publisher summary describes an older policy analysis. It is not a current budget estimate, a current legal finding, or a causal evaluation of a proposed formula.

Questions that remain

How would current funding options redistribute resources and support outcomes?
