Mitigating Educator Workforce Impacts Through Coaching

Anne Cash and research team

Educator workforceInstructional improvement

What the study found

The summary reports a positive relationship between program participation and retention, together with shifts in coaching time and topics.

Considerations for practice

Document the content and intensity of beginning-teacher coaching, not participation alone.

Limitations and interpretation

Participation may reflect teacher or district differences. The preliminary project page does not supply a full identification strategy or isolate effective coaching components.

Questions that remain

Which coaching practices support teachers in different school contexts?
