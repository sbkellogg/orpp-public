Implementing a Community School Framework in Rural Schools

Amy Swain and research team

Family engagementStudent supportsSafety & well-being

What the study found

The project reports increased family engagement, event participation, and staff morale during its initial pilot.

Considerations for practice

Use family listening sessions and community partnerships to identify locally relevant supports.

Limitations and interpretation

The public account is a local preliminary summary without a comparison design. Reported improvements cannot be attributed solely to the community school framework.

Questions that remain

Which components transfer to other rural communities?
