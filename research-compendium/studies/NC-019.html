Working paperPriority 6NC-019 · 2023

ESSER III Investments in North Carolina: A Preliminary Analysis of PRC 181 and PRC 182

Erin W. Manuel; Camille N. Mikkelsen

School financeAI & technology

What the study found

Spending plans emphasized technology, personnel, academic recovery, and health and safety. Quantitative and qualitative analyses described differences in planned priorities.

Considerations for practice

Use plans to identify investments that need follow-up implementation and outcome evaluation.

Limitations and interpretation

Plans are not verified expenditures or impacts. This study does not establish returns on ESSER spending or provide evidence about AI tools.

Questions that remain

How did actual spending and implementation compare with plans?

Study at a glance

Design
Mixed-methods document analysis
NC setting & population
ESSER III applications from 112 traditional LEAs and 177 charter public school units submitted before November 2021.
