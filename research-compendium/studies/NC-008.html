The Effect of Immigration Enforcement on School Engagement: Evidence from 287(g) Programs in North Carolina

Laura Bellows

AttendanceFamily engagement

What the study found

The analysis found lower attendance after program activation, with increases in chronic absence, but no detected achievement effect.

Considerations for practice

Investigate community and family barriers when interpreting changes in attendance.

Limitations and interpretation

The historical policy setting differs from current enforcement contexts. Chronic absence used a 15-day threshold, so rates are not directly comparable with today’s 10% measure.

Questions that remain

Which locally appropriate family supports protect school engagement?

Study at a glance

Design
Triple-difference policy analysis
NC setting & population
NC counties approved for 287(g) partnerships compared with counties that applied but were not approved.
