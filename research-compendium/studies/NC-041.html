Why Do Students Participate (or Not) in Dual Enrollment?

Bryan C. Hutchins; Nina Arshavsky; Julie Edmunds; Laura Rosof

Postsecondary & CTE

What the study found

Participants valued savings, challenge, and career exploration. Awareness, advising, remaining costs, transportation, scheduling, and course availability could limit participation.

Considerations for practice

Address practical access barriers alongside tuition and eligibility.

Limitations and interpretation

A small purposive qualitative sample explains experiences, not prevalence or causal effects. Interviews reflect 2020 conditions.

Questions that remain

Which changes remove barriers for eligible nonparticipants?

Study at a glance

Design
Qualitative interviews and student focus groups
NC setting & population
2020 visits to four NC high schools; 27 staff and 63 students, including eligible nonparticipants.
