CTE-Focused Dual Enrollment: Participation and Outcomes

Julie A. Edmunds; Fatih Unlu; Brian Phillips; Bryan C. Hutchins; Christine Mulhern

Postsecondary & CTEAdvanced learning

What the study found

About 9% participated. Participation was positively associated with college credits, high-school graduation, and college enrollment; participation disparities were smaller than in college-transfer dual enrollment.

Considerations for practice

Examine CTE dual enrollment as one pathway to broaden college access.

Limitations and interpretation

Participation was not randomly assigned. The abstract supports associations and does not show that every CTE pathway produces the same benefits.

Questions that remain

Which credentials and programs lead to sustained completion and employment?

Study at a glance

Design
Quasi-experimental analysis
NC setting & population
North Carolina students participating in CTE dual enrollment in grades 11–12.
