2026 North Carolina Teacher Working Conditions Survey: Preliminary Statewide Findings and Implications for School, District, and State Leaders

Shaun Kellogg

Working ConditionsEducator workforceSafety & well-beingInstructional improvementAssessment & accountability

What the study found

Teachers reported strong school commitment, with 84.3% planning to stay at their school. Time, student conduct, and professional support were weaker areas; ratings differed by intended career plans.

Considerations for practice

Use local results and educator feedback to prioritize planning time, leadership, and support needs.

Limitations and interpretation

Preliminary, descriptive self-reports do not establish causation or actual retention. Statewide averages mask local variation; charter participation was lower. Comment modeling is exploratory.

Questions that remain

Do reported conditions and professional plans predict subsequent workforce and student outcomes?

Study at a glance

Design
Descriptive survey analysis; exploratory comment topic modeling
NC setting & population
Statewide licensed school-based educators; 102,640 responses and a 90.5% response rate.
