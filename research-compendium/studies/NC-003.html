Student-Level Attendance Patterns Across Three Post-Pandemic Years

Tom Swiderski; Sarah Crittenden Fuller; Kevin C. Bastian

AttendanceData systems

What the study found

The share chronically absent at least once over three years rose from 17% to 38%; persistent chronic absence across all three years rose from 2.4% to 9.6%.

Considerations for practice

Distinguish occasional absence from repeated, intensive needs using student histories across years.

Limitations and interpretation

The sample excludes high school students and requires observation across three years. Descriptive changes do not establish causes.

Questions that remain

How should support intensity vary with the persistence of absence?

Study at a glance

Design
Longitudinal descriptive analysis
NC setting & population
NC students in grades K–6 at baseline, followed for three years; 2016–19 versus 2021–24.
