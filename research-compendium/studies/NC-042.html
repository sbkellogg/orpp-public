CARES Intensive Intervention Coaching Pilot: Findings in NCDPI’s 2025 Low-Performing Schools Report

North Carolina Department of Public Instruction; summarizing EPIC evaluation findings

Instructional improvementEducator workforce

What the study found

Educators reported stronger instructional and leadership routines. School exits from low-performing status were unstable across years and driven largely by growth-status changes.

Considerations for practice

Plan for sustained coaching and examine outcome trajectories beyond a single accountability designation.

Limitations and interpretation

The report summarizes emerging findings and does not present a full comparison design. Descriptive status changes do not establish coaching effects or effects of ESSER funding overall.

Questions that remain

Are gains sustained after intensive coaching ends?
