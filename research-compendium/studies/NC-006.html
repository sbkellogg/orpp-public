Ending Early Grade Suspensions

Ezra Karger; Sarah Komisarow

Safety & well-beingStudent supports

What the study found

The reform reduced suspension probability by 1.4 percentage points and narrowed the Black–White suspension gap. Average test scores and disciplinary infractions did not worsen.

Considerations for practice

Examine alternatives to exclusion alongside classroom outcomes and disciplinary disparities.

Limitations and interpretation

Findings concern one district and early grades. The working-paper version does not establish effects of every suspension policy or of high-school reforms.

Questions that remain

Which implementation supports sustain equitable discipline over time?

Study at a glance

Design
Quasi-experimental policy evaluation
NC setting & population
Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools; a policy limiting out-of-school suspensions in grades K–2.
