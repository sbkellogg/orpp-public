The Causal Effect of Student Absences Post Pandemic: Evidence from Three School Systems

Yu Hung Yaow; Seth Gershenson; David Blazar; Ethan Hutt

AttendanceMathematics

What the study found

Across the study settings, absences remained harmful to test performance after the pandemic, although estimated effects were modestly smaller than before.

Considerations for practice

Treat attendance as a continuing learning concern while checking how effects vary by setting.

Limitations and interpretation

This is a working-paper abstract. Pooled findings are not NC-only estimates; the attainment results described for Maryland should not be transferred to NC.

Questions that remain

Do NC effects differ by grade, subject, or type of absence?

Study at a glance
