Math Acceleration in Elementary School: Access and Effects on Student Outcomes

Steven W. Hemelt; Matthew A. Lenard

MathematicsAdvanced learning

What the study found

Girls were less likely to be nominated and qualify than similarly performing boys. Acceleration effects differed by gender, including evidence of short-term mathematics knowledge decay for girls.

Considerations for practice

Review nomination, qualification, and continued participation separately when expanding access.

Limitations and interpretation

One district’s elementary single-subject acceleration policy is distinct from statewide middle-grade automatic enrollment. The findings should not be treated as universal acceleration effects.

Questions that remain

What supports help students remain successful after acceleration?
