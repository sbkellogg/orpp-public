Project READS NC: Understanding LETRS Implementation

Paola Pilonieta and Project READS NC team

LiteracyInstructional improvementWorking Conditions

What the study found

The project summary reports knowledge and implementation challenges, including limited preparation time and perceived gaps in administrative support.

Considerations for practice

Pair professional learning with protected planning time and support for classroom application.

Limitations and interpretation

The source presents preliminary survey findings. Perceptions do not establish LETRS effects on student achievement; a final analysis was not reviewed.

Questions that remain

How do coaching and implementation supports change instruction and learning?

Agenda questions this work informs

Priority 3EmergingRA-015

Implementation studies identify barriers that outcome averages can miss.

Implementation

LETRS observations, CCP interviews, and preliminary coaching reports describe how support and access work in practice. Such evidence helps define improvement questions but does not establish that a proposed change causes better outcomes. ATR annual case studies add district-specific evidence about roles and coaching time.

Professional learning needs support for classroom application.

Professional learning and leadership
