The Growing Segmentation of the Charter School Sector in North Carolina

Helen F. Ladd; Charles T. Clotfelter; John B. Holbein

School choice & charter

What the study found

The study documented increasing sector segmentation and racial imbalance. For the historical period studied, charter schools were not more effective on average than traditional public schools.

Considerations for practice

Consider enrollment composition, access, and school variation when interpreting sector averages.

Limitations and interpretation

Although published within the search window, the data are much older. These findings should not be presented as a current estimate of charter-school performance.

Questions that remain

How have access and outcomes changed under more recent policies?
