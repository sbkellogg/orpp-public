Keeping Students Safe in the Digital World: School Monitoring Technologies

Florence Martin and research team

AI & technologySafety & well-being

What the study found

Participants perceived content filtering more favorably than protection against cyberbullying or self-harm. The summary also reports substantial unmet teacher training needs.

Considerations for practice

Evaluate purpose, staff preparation, privacy, and response processes when reviewing monitoring tools.

Limitations and interpretation

Perceived effectiveness is not demonstrated prevention. The summary does not establish effects of AI-supported instruction or the accuracy of individual monitoring products.

Questions that remain

What independent outcome and privacy evidence supports specific uses?
