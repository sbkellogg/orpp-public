Leading Workforce Effectiveness: Inhibiting and Supporting Factors

Laura Lunsford and research team

Educator workforceWorking Conditions

What the study found

Interviewees described student behavior, leadership turnover, support-service shortages, and local incentives as relevant to teacher retention.

Considerations for practice

Explore local organizational conditions and support capacity in retention planning.

Limitations and interpretation

Purposeful selection and interview accounts do not establish statewide prevalence or causal effects. Findings remain preliminary in the reviewed summary.

Questions that remain

Which locally feasible changes improve retention over time?

Study at a glance

Design
Qualitative interviews
NC setting & population
Eight rural NC LEAs selected from areas with relatively high or low teacher attrition.
