What Happens When You Combine High School and College? The Impact of Early Colleges on Postsecondary Performance and Completion

SERVE Center research team

Postsecondary & CTEAdvanced learning

What the study found

Lottery-offered students more often earned a postsecondary credential (44.3% versus 33.0%), particularly an associate degree. The overall bachelor’s completion difference at six years was not statistically significant.

Considerations for practice

Consider the complete early-college model, including academic and student supports.

Limitations and interpretation

Results apply to applicants and participating lottery schools. They do not isolate dual-credit coursework from other features of the model.

Questions that remain

How do effects transfer to other schools and current student cohorts?

Study at a glance

Design
Randomized lottery study
NC setting & population
1,687 applicants to 12 North Carolina early colleges; follow-up through six years after grade 12.
