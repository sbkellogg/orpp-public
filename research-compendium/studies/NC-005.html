School-Based Healthcare and Absenteeism: Evidence from Telemedicine

Sarah Komisarow; Steven W. Hemelt

Student supportsAttendanceSafety & well-being

What the study found

Access to school telemedicine was associated with a 2.5 percentage-point reduction in chronic absence and about 0.8 fewer days absent; effects were concentrated among male students.

Considerations for practice

Consider healthcare access when investigating local attendance barriers.

Limitations and interpretation

Three rural districts do not represent every NC context. Clinic access is not the same as an individual student receiving care.

Questions that remain

What staffing, costs, and local partnerships support replication?
