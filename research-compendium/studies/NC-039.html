The Role of Course Delivery Modality in Dual Enrollment

Julie A. Edmunds; Brian Phillips; Christine Mulhern; Nina Arshavsky; Bryan C. Hutchins

Postsecondary & CTEInstructional improvement

What the study found

All three delivery groups had favorable postsecondary enrollment and credential estimates versus nonparticipants. Course pass rates differed; online CTE had the lowest pass rates.

Considerations for practice

Consider course completion supports alongside access when selecting delivery formats.

Limitations and interpretation

Only the author abstract was reviewed. Comparisons are with nonparticipants, not randomized head-to-head modality assignments; results do not establish the best format for each student.

Questions that remain

Which delivery supports improve success for students facing access barriers?

Study at a glance

Design
Propensity-weighted outcomes analysis with qualitative evidence
