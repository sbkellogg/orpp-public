Research report2026 · NC-033

The Selection and Evaluation of Advanced Teachers: Summative Report

Sarah Bausell; James Birkett; Duncan Culbreth; Brayan Diaz; Shaun Kellogg; Emily Thrasher; Robin Anderson; Lam Pham; Tamara Young; Kari Colley; Sarah Dyson; Caitlin Kearney; Liv Rau; Matthew G. Springer; David Stuit

Districts used varied, multistage selection processes; evaluation practices were less developed and often missed teacher-leadership responsibilities. No single selection measure consistently predicted both student achievement and supported-teacher retention. The study developed the Adult Leadership Teacher Evaluation Rubric (ALTER).

Agency report2025 · NC-043

Advanced Teaching Roles Class Size Report

North Carolina Department of Public Instruction

The report proposes team-level student-to-teacher ratios that account for leadership release time. A small, one-year Classroom Excellence analysis found no clear class-size relationship with teacher growth scores.

Evaluation report2024 · NC-044

2024 Advanced Teaching Roles Annual Evaluation Report

Sarah Bausell; Shaun Kellogg; Emily Thrasher; Lam Pham; Tamara Young

School-level estimates were positive and significant in mathematics, while overall ELA, science, and teacher-turnover differences were not significant. Gains were stronger in earlier implementation cohorts. District case studies identified role clarity and release-time challenges.
