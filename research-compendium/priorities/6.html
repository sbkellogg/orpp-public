Priority 6

Policy Design, Resource Allocation, and Governance

Consider how funding, access, and organizational policies shape opportunities and outcomes.

The agenda’s central question

How do policies governing educational resources, student access, workforce flexibility, and school operations influence educational opportunities, organizational capacity, and student outcomes, and what are the potential consequences of alternative policy approaches?

Priority 6EmergingRA-057

Flexibility is a means whose implementation matters.

Workforce and organizational flexibility

Restart and annual ATR evaluation findings vary by model and context. The separate ATR summative study describes variation in teacher-leader selection and evaluation and offers exploratory predictive findings. It does not establish that changing selection rules causes better outcomes.
