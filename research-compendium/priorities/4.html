Priority 4

Attendance, Safety, Health, and School Climate

Connect evidence on engagement, school climate, health, family relationships, and discipline.

The agenda’s central question

How do school climate, safety, health and behavioral health supports, family engagement, and organizational conditions interact to influence attendance, behavior, well-being, and academic outcomes?

Priority 4SubstantialRA-041

Attendance matters, and its causes and remedies differ.

Attendance

Several NC studies document persistent absence and its relationship to learning. Rural healthcare and community-policy studies examine particular pathways; they do not establish one universal attendance intervention.

School climate
