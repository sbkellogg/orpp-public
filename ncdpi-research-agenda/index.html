About the Research Agenda

NCDPI’s research agenda identifies priority questions and areas of interest to guide partnerships and inform policy and practice. Coordinated by the Office of Research and Promising Practices, this directory brings together six research priorities, supporting questions, areas of interest, cross-cutting perspectives, enabling data needs, and policy-responsive research opportunities.

Explore the priorities below to find research aligned with your interests and identify potential collaborating offices or program sponsors. Office connections are starting points for discussion; specific roles and sponsorship should be agreed with the relevant office. Consult the NCDPI staff directory for current contacts, or download the agenda crosswalk to review entries, topics, office connections, and resources.

How the Agenda Was Developed

Developed through the SLDS grant. This research agenda was developed as part of NCDPI’s 2023 Statewide Longitudinal Data Systems (SLDS) grant, Enhancing the North Carolina Data Integration and Consolidated Reporting Systems. Awarded through the U.S. Department of Education’s Institute of Education Sciences, the grant supports data system modernization and stronger use of longitudinal data for research, evaluation, and decision-making. The award runs from September 2023 through September 2027 (award number R372A230035).

Listening to data users. The agenda builds on work to identify research and data priorities for North Carolina’s statewide longitudinal data system. In November and December 2025, the Office of Research and Promising Practices conducted interviews with NCDPI staff across program areas to understand the decisions they support, the outcomes they need to track, and the questions that better-connected data could help answer. Related feedback from school leaders highlighted needs for timely information, easier access, and less duplicative data preparation.

Organizing shared questions. Research questions and program interests were consolidated into six connected priorities, each with a central question and supporting questions. Cross-cutting perspectives and data needs were identified alongside the research topics, connecting the questions to the measures, linkages, and reporting capabilities needed to study them.

Connecting research to decisions. The agenda provides a common framework for research that can support NCDPI programs and the goals of Achieving Educational Excellence, North Carolina’s strategic plan. Policy and program examples illustrate opportunities for inquiry. Individual studies, office responsibilities, and sponsorship arrangements are established through discussion with the relevant offices and the research partnership and data request process.
