Research Priority Areas

NCDPI’s research agenda identifies priority questions and areas of interest to guide partnerships and inform policy and practice. Coordinated by the Office of Research and Promising Practices, this directory brings together six research priorities, supporting questions, areas of interest, cross-cutting perspectives, enabling data needs, and policy-responsive research opportunities. The agenda’s development was supported by NCDPI’s Statewide Longitudinal Data Systems (SLDS) grant and informed by cross-office interviews to identify shared research and data priorities.

Explore the priorities below to find research aligned with your interests. Consult the NCDPI staff directory for current contacts, or download the agenda crosswalk to review entries, topics, office connections, and resources.

Priority 1

Student Pathways, Access, and Outcomes

Trace student journeys from early learning through postsecondary transitions, examining access, achievement, and opportunities for success.
