Offices & Leadership

Explore NCDPI offices and leaders to identify potential collaborators and program sponsors for your research. The connections listed here are starting points for discussion; sponsorship should be agreed with the relevant office. Leadership names are sourced from public NCDPI office pages and directories. Consult the NCDPI staff directory for current contacts.

NCDPI Sponsorship. Data requests will only be approved when the proposed research is sponsored by a program area within the North Carolina Department of Public Instruction. Sponsorship should be documented through a DPI division, office, or program lead (for example, the Office of Research & Promising Practices). Review the Research Partnerships: Data Requests & Letters of Support guidance for approval criteria and the request process.
