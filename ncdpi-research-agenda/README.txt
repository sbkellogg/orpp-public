NCDPI Research & Policy Priorities — Research Directory

Open index.html in a modern browser. The search and filters work without internet access. Keep orpp-logo.png alongside index.html to display the office logo. External NCDPI and legislative links require internet access.

Contents:
- index.html: main research agenda with search and filters.
- offices.html: office directory and sponsorship guidance.
- agenda-development.html: agenda background, development process, and sources.
- directory-data.js: shared research and office information used by the research and office pages; keep aligned with agenda_data.json.
- styles.css: shared styles for all pages.
- offices.js: office directory rendering.
- agenda_crosswalk.csv: all entries with suggested collaborating offices and links; edit in Excel.
- agenda_data.json: structured source data (developer-friendly).
- orpp-logo.png: office logo used in the header.

Important:
- The research questions and areas of interest are drawn from NCDPI_Research_Questions_and_Priorities_Integrated_TWC_2026.docx. Questions from the previous standalone TWC inventory were consolidated into the standing research agenda rather than duplicated as a separate question bank.
- Additional 2026 legislative illustrations are explicitly labeled and should not be interpreted as an approved research portfolio.
- Office-to-topic relationships are suggestions, not assignments approved by office directors.
- The office leaders listed are based on publicly accessible NCDPI materials checked Oct. 24, 2026 and should be rechecked before public deployment.
- To publish as an official site, complete internal content/ownership review, accessibility testing, and the agency web approval process.
